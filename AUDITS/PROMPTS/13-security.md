# Audit — Security & Threat Model

Audite auth, authorization, sessions, OAuth, API, uploads, profils publics, missions, submissions, squads et admin.

Cherche au minimum:
Broken Access Control, IDOR, privilege escalation, auth bypass, session fixation, CSRF, XSS stored/reflected, injection, SSRF, open redirect, path traversal, malicious uploads, rate-limit bypass, enumeration, information disclosure et secrets leakage.

Pour chaque finding:
Severity | Evidence | Attack scenario | Impact | Exploitability | Fix.

Ne fais aucune exploitation destructive.
